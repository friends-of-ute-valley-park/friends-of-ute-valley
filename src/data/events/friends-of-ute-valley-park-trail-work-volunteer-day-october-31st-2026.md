Join Friends of Ute Valley Park and Rocky Mountain Field Institute (RMFI) on Saturday, October 31, from 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. to extend rock armoring on the Black side of the Black and Blue Loop.

## Meeting location

Meet Friends of Ute Valley Park and RMFI at the South Rockrimmon Trailhead, also known as the HP lot, on Ute Valley Trail. [View RMFI's meeting-location map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jmCFXfcm22j2NbJF6).

## Registration and participation

[Register through RMFI](https://www.rmfi.org/civicrm-event/2693) by Friday, October 30, at 3 p.m. If you miss online registration, a paper waiver will be available at check-in. Please plan to stay for the full workday.

Participants must be at least 16 years old. Minors must be accompanied by an adult.

This is moderately strenuous work involving tools and moving rock. Expect a half-mile hike each way while carrying tools. Contact [Calla Balliett at RMFI](mailto:calla@rmfi.org) before registering if you have accessibility questions or need accommodations.

## What to bring

- Long pants and sturdy, closed-toe shoes are required.
- Bring work gloves, eye protection, and clothing suited to the weather. Gloves will be available to borrow.
- Carry a backpack with at least 2 liters of water, snacks, sunscreen, and any personal essentials. Keep your hands free to carry tools on the hike.

RMFI will provide tools and a small snack. See [RMFI's volunteer guidance](https://www.rmfi.org/learn-about-volunteering) for more preparation details.
